# Concept 06: Blue Ridge

[Open homepage](../../prototypes/ridge.html) | [Store preview](../../prototypes/store.html?design=ridge) | [Project and business plan](../../prototypes/plan.html) | [Comparison gallery](../../prototypes/index.html)

## Thesis

Lead with the feeling of East Tennessee while making a small product range
unusually easy to scan. An expansive blue panorama and oversized serif headline
establish place and character. A structured product ledger turns that atmosphere
into useful honey, soap, and candle discovery.

This is an original regional illustration, **not an actual apiary, mountain
view, public pickup site, map, or claim that the business is located on the
Blue Ridge**. The confirmed locality is Madisonville, Tennessee. Real product
status and pickup arrangements matter more than scenic branding.

## Owner context: planning-only boundary

Soap is the most grounded near-term product in the owner-supplied planning
context; candles remain planned. Consumer honey is a future retail concept only:
no real stock, preorder, or harvest allocation is offered. Future harvest or B2B
commitments do not establish consumer inventory. Operational scale, production
ownership, and partnership context are not approved public marketing and must
not become homepage claims without separate approval.

## Palette, type, and graphic language

| Role | Choice and application |
|---|---|
| Paper | `#f3f5f7`, cool neutral main ground |
| Ink | `#203b56`, primary text, action, rules, footer |
| Blue | `#315c78`, local-story chapter and landscape anchors |
| Sky | `#dae6ed`, pickup chapter and illustration ground |
| Cream | `#f3ecdb`, landscape light and text over the blue story chapter |
| Display | Georgia/system serif, oversized, normal weight, occasional italic |
| Body/utility | Arial/Helvetica/system sans; indexed product labels |
| Motifs | Panoramic layers, horizon-like rules, product ledger, no ornamental honeycomb |

Calculated solid-color ratios: ink/paper approximately **10.55:1**, ink/sky
**9.07:1**, blue/cream **6.08:1**. Text is not placed over changing landscape
detail; mood and information have separate layers.

The centered masthead, full-bleed panorama, and horizontal product ledger give
this concept a different reading rhythm from the market shelf and product
studio. Landscape does not move services ahead of goods.

## Customer acquisition rationale: a testable hypothesis

The [existing research](../01-market-and-design-research.md) observes concrete
locality/contact in the Madisonville producer listing and origin/making context
at Appalachian Bee and Big Island Bees. It does not establish a preference for
landscapes, blue palettes, or serif typography.

**Hypothesis:** someone arriving from a verified local/social referral will
recognize an East Tennessee point of view, then quickly compare three
plain-language product families. The ledger may favor shoppers who want useful
information rather than a dense retail grid. This remains a proposition to
test, not a conversion claim.

Apply the [shared evaluation rubric](01-meadow-and-amber.md#shared-evaluation-rubric).
Ask a local buyer to locate soap, open the product preview, and explain what can
actually be bought now. Ask whether the scene is a real property and where they
would collect goods. Also test a grower finding pollination. Misreading the scene
as an actual pickup destination is a design failure, regardless of visual appeal.

## Coordinated collection-to-cart journey

Every shared commerce destination uses **`design=ridge`**.

1. Hero, header, and footer actions enter the
   [store preview](../../prototypes/store.html?design=ridge).
2. Ledger links open the complete
   [seasonal honey](../../prototypes/product.html?design=ridge&product=seasonal-honey),
   [honey + oat soap](../../prototypes/product.html?design=ridge&product=honey-oat-soap),
   and [beeswax candle](../../prototypes/product.html?design=ridge&product=beeswax-candle)
   product previews. The homepage contains no fake quantity/cart controls.
3. Shared product/store pages continue to the
   [sample cart](../../prototypes/cart.html?design=ridge), preserving the theme.
   Sample pricing and stock remain explicitly fictional; cart activity is not a
   reservation, payment, submission, or order.
4. The owner-facing
   [inventory preview](../../prototypes/inventory.html?design=ridge) supports a
   catalog discussion, not a claim about actual inventory or a live admin
   system.

The real launch is local-pickup first. Availability, payment, location, and timing
must be confirmed through verified direct contact. A private pickup agreement
does not imply a public shop. Shipping belongs to a later verified operational
phase.

## Mobile, accessibility, and resilience

- At desktop the masthead has locality/wordmark/shop columns, followed by a
  full-width visible navigation row. Hero copy is centered above the panorama.
- At 760px and below, the wordmark moves above the locality and shop link.
  Navigation wraps; no hamburger or JavaScript is needed.
- The panorama is intentionally cropped on phones. Its caption remains readable
  outside the image and does not lose the imagined-site disclaimer.
- The four-part desktop ledger moves through a compact tablet layout into
  art-and-title entries with full-width description and product action at 480px.
  It is not a horizontally scrolling table.
- Pickup/story panels and service/contact layouts stack as width decreases.
  Information stays in source order; no hover-only content exists.
- Semantic sections, one h1, a skip link, labeled navigation, visible focus, fixed
  image dimensions, and key links at least 44 CSS px tall support core tasks.
- There is no parallax, animation, script, remote font, embedded feed, external
  asset request, or location/map integration. Reduced motion disables smooth
  scrolling.
- Check 320/375/768/1440px, keyboard, and zoom. At narrow widths particularly
  inspect the serif headline and product names for awkward wrapping.

## Future SEO intent

Keep this review **noindex**. If selected, publish a single canonical production
direction with accurate Madisonville identity, genuine product/collection
pages, product-specific descriptions, and verified pickup/contact information.
Do not turn the visual concept name into misleading location or geographic
service claims.

Separate actual removal and farm-pollination pages should describe confirmed
coverage, intake, and responsibilities. Do not publish a fictional address,
opening hours, view, map, offer/review schema, eco certification, therapeutic
claim, or shipping availability. Only approved business and product facts can
support future structured data. Neither local rank nor rich results are assured.

## Tradeoffs and mitigations

| Opportunity | Tradeoff / mitigation |
|---|---|
| Distinct regional atmosphere without rustic cliches | Scenic art can imply a real property; keep the caption and explicit story disclaimer |
| Strong, memorable display hierarchy | Large headline and scene consume vertical space; keep the store CTA above the panorama |
| Product ledger favors comparison | Less immersive product imagery; reserve full detail/imagery for the shared product pages |
| Blue stands apart from expected honey gold | May feel less immediately honey-related; name product families in the opening copy |
| Clear services remain available | Secondary placement adds scroll; retain visible navigation and separate assessment links |
| Local-first handoff supports an honest small launch | Operations remain unverified; never replace the pickup caveat with a fabricated map/address |

## Open owner decisions

Confirm whether regional landscape is the desired expression and whether the
"Blue Ridge" review label could mislead their audience. Approve actual product
range, ingredient/material details, sizes, labels, whether honey can enter a
separately approved retail catalog, and planned candle viability. Set real stock,
pricing, payment, and private pickup
arrangements. Supply the owner story, actual product/process imagery,
phone/email, and owned social links. Confirm removal/pollination coverage,
capability, capacity, structural-work/repair scope, and safe intake. Shipping
and public visits are not approved.
