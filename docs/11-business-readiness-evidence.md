# Business readiness: evidence and confirmation gates

Research date: **2026-10-28**. Status: **source review, not a legal opinion, registration service or compliance approval**.

## What was and was not verified

Three federal guidance pages were directly read. Tennessee/local official sources were located, but access failures prevented verification of their operative requirements. **Treat Tennessee/local entries below as confirmation destinations, not established rules.**

No tax rate, license fee, exemption threshold, mandatory LLC conclusion, apiary renewal period, honey yield or revenue forecast was established. Existing soap sales and two hives do not prove every future product/channel is covered, nor does this research establish that current operations are noncompliant.

## Verified federal guidance

| Source | Supported observation | Business-plan consequence |
|---|---|---|
| [FDA - Soap FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/frequently-asked-questions-soap) | Classification depends on composition, cleansing action and intended use/marketing: "all three conditions must be met"; qualifying soap is "labeled and marketed only for use as soap" | Record the actual formula and claims. The product name alone does not determine true-soap/cosmetic/drug status. Qualifying true soap is under CPSC rather than FDA; cosmetic or treatment claims can change the analysis. |
| [FDA - Small Businesses and Homemade Cosmetics](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/resources-industry-cosmetics/small-businesses-homemade-cosmetics-fact-sheet) | "MoCRA exempts certain small businesses from facility registration and product listing" with conditions/excluded categories; safety and labeling responsibilities are not generally waived | Review the actual business/products. Do not assume either universal registration or a universal small-business exemption. Home production is not a safety waiver. |
| [CPSC - Candle business guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Candles) | Distinguishes mandatory metal-core-wick restrictions, conditional hazardous-substance labeling and "industry consensus standards (also known as voluntary standards)" | Assess the actual candle/wick/container/claims and relevant safety guidance. Do not present every ASTM standard as mandatory, copy standards text, or conclude that a candle needs no safety review. |

FDA also discusses safety substantiation/records and cosmetic address-labeling rules. Its fact sheet says a post-office box or website alone is not adequate for the labeling requirement described; rules/exceptions and actual classification need suitable review. **Pickup privacy does not justify inventing a label address or omitting legally required information.** Obtain a valid approach without automatically publishing a home/apiary on the website.

Page revision dates and current ASTM editions were not verified in this bounded pass. Recheck current official guidance at the actual product/channel decision; a retrieval date is not a guarantee of legal currency.

## Product and channel review gates

| Gate | Decision/evidence needed | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Actual operating location | City-limit/jurisdiction status, permitted activity, pickup/public-access arrangement | Q04-Q05 |
| Actual soap classification | Formula, cleansing action, intended use, label and website/social claims | Q02/Q11 |
| Honey and derivatives | Current applicable rules for the specific product/process and each sales channel | Q01/Q11 |
| Apiary | Applicable registration and operational obligations confirmed with TDA | Q16 |
| Candles | Actual wick/container/composition, safety/label review and suitable testing/standards decisions | Q11/Q13 |
| Markets/wholesale | Venue/product eligibility, documentation, handling/sampling if any, insurance, payment/complaint responsibilities | Q11/Q15 |
| Pickup | Safe actual location, privacy/accessibility, timing, ready notification, no-show/cancel/return process | Q05 |
| Later shipping | Product/channel eligibility, destination obligations, packaging, breakage/returns, true economics | Q14 |

A permitted formula is not proof that every market, pickup venue, wholesale arrangement or interstate shipment is permitted. An exemption for one honey product/process does not automatically cover added ingredients or another product category.

Maintain supplier/batch records, quality checks and a complaint/withdrawal process as practical risk controls. Do not represent every recommended record as a separately verified statutory requirement.

## Record confirmation without exposing private data

For each applicable gate, privately retain the authority/adviser, dated guidance or document, applicability reasoning, responsible owner and next review. In this repository, record only the non-sensitive decision and whether approved public wording may be used. No account credentials, private customer data or confidential commercial terms belong in public source.

Use the [partner workbook](10-partner-workbook.md) to assign decisions and the [business plan](09-local-business-launch-plan.md) to sequence them. The real business launch requires owner/qualified confirmation; completing a website or prototype does not satisfy these gates.
