## Decision record format

For each question, record:

```text
Question ID:
Decision or remaining uncertainty:
Evidence / qualified confirmation:
Responsible owner:
Next action and review date:
Approved for public copy? yes / no / only these facts:
Affected product, operating procedure, plan or website section:
```

Use "proposed" until the relevant owner agrees. Do not mark an obligation satisfied because a prototype looks finished. Do not put credentials, private customer data, personal finances or confidential agreement details into publicly hosted files.
