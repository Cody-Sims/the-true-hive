The work behind
the everyday.

A local beginning.
Not a borrowed history.

The True Hive brings together soap-making and beekeeping in Madisonville, Tennessee. This editorial direction makes room for the real people, practical work and product details behind that local business.

What belongs here next is the owner's approved account: why they make, what the actual process looks like, and which goods are genuinely ready. No founding date, multigenerational story or testimonial has been invented.

This is proposed editorial space, not an archive of published journal articles.
