Field notes / Proposed sample content

Know a little more
about what you choose.

Good product information begins with questions, not sweeping claims. These are the details we would explain for an approved collection.

01

In the jar

Identify the actual honey, format and verified origin. A flowering illustration is not evidence of floral source or a harvest promise.

02

In the bar

Show the full real ingredient list and explain the approved formula in plain language. No health or treatment claims belong here.

03

In the candle

State the wax composition, wick, vessel and safe-use instructions. The product facts should do the talking.
