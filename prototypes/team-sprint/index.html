Website & office · Ready

Make shopping guidance consistent

Help first-time visitors tell what they can do now without having to reach the cart.

Checklist

  1. Compare the main actions on the home, collection, and product pages.
  2. Use one consistent next step that matches current ordering availability.
  3. Check the page sequence on a phone and with a keyboard.

Handoff

Ask the hands-on lead to read the revised copy and confirm it matches what customers can expect.

Done when

All three pages describe the same next step and none promises an order that cannot be placed.

Hands-on · Ready

Verify soap format details

Give the website lead a reliable source for what each finished format is and how it should be described.

Checklist

  1. Match each finished format to its printed label.
  2. Confirm the net weight for each format and flag any mismatch separately.
  3. Collect verified format names and label references in one note.

Handoff

Send the checked facts to the website lead for Update the soap listings.

Done when

Every selected format has verified details or is explicitly flagged for review.

Hands-on · Ready

Check which products are ready for review

Keep the collection clear about what is ready to show and what is still an idea.

Checklist

  1. Mark each item as a finished sample, a future concept, or not yet confirmed.
  2. Flag anything that still needs a label, photo, or approval.

Handoff

Give the website lead the approved customer-facing product status, not internal counts or commitments.

Done when

All items reviewed have a clear status, and unconfirmed items are not presented as available.

Hands-on · Ready

Take real product photos

Help shoppers recognize the finished item they would pick up.

Checklist

  1. Take one clear photo per approved sample in consistent light.
  2. Name each file by format and check it for private details before sharing.

Handoff

Send cleared, labeled photos to the website lead for Update the soap listings. Leave any format without a usable image flagged.

Done when

The website lead can match each approved photo to the correct format without guessing.

Website & office · Waiting

Update the soap listings

Wait for the confirmed soap details and approved photos before editing product facts.

Checklist

  1. Use the verified weights and format names from the hands-on lead.
  2. Pair each format with the matching approved photo.
  3. On a phone, switch between the formats and check that all three details agree.

Handoff

Needs Verify soap format details and Take real product photos. Ask the hands-on lead to confirm the draft before publishing.

Done when

The displayed name, weight, and photo all match the selected format.

Website & office · Waiting

Write simple pickup instructions

Set expectations before anyone travels, without turning a private location into a public listing.

Checklist

  1. Confirm the staffed pickup window and when a customer should expect a message.
  2. State what to do if the timing changes and who to contact.
  3. Keep precise directions out of public copy unless approved.

Handoff

Wait for the hands-on lead to check the arrival experience and confirm a workable schedule.

Done when

In a practice walkthrough, a first-time customer knows when to wait, when to travel, and how to reschedule.
